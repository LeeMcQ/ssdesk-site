How to show hymns with music on the projector

Updated 2026-11-20

Sabbath Service Desk shows a hymn one stanza at a time and can play the hymn music from the PC with no internet, so a small church without a pianist can still sing. The words follow the music.

Show a hymn

  1. Open the Hymns tab, or type hymn 73 in the Go to box at the top.
  2. Find the hymn by number or by words. Choose the hymnbook at the top of the list.
  3. Choose which stanzas to sing if you do not sing them all (for example 1, 2 and 4).
  4. Press Present to show the words only, or Present & play for words and music.
  5. The title comes first, then one stanza per slide. The chorus is repeated after each stanza for you.

Words and music together

  • With Present & play, the music plays an introduction, then one verse of music per stanza you chose, with the real ending on the last one.
  • The words change two seconds before the music reaches them, so people can read ahead. Change this in Hymns → ⚙ Hymn options → Words change earlier (0–5 seconds).
  • Press → or ← at any time to take over. Tapping a stanza while the hymn plays changes only the words; the music does not restart.
  • Press M to play or pause the music. The tempo can be changed.

Make it sound right in your hall

The setup asks what the device is (church PC, laptop or phone), how big the hall is and what the music is heard through (sound desk, small speakers or a TV), and chooses the sound. Change it later under Hymns → ⚙ Hymn options: sound presets (Best, Balanced, Light, Large church with organ, Small room and clear), room echo and an equaliser. Play a test lets you hear it before the service.

Hymns in your language

English and Afrikaans hymnbooks are built in. Hymnbooks in more languages, such as isiXhosa, isiZulu, Sesotho, Setswana, Shona, Tshivenda, Swahili, French, Portuguese and Spanish, are added once from Add a hymnbook and then work offline. Where a hymn shares its tune with the English hymnal, the music plays for it too. Your church's own songs and choruses can be added in the Shared Hymns book of each language.

Tip: on a phone or an old PC, choose the Light sound preset. On a PC that is fast enough, the setup's 2-minute load test tells you if it is ready.
