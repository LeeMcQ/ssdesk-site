How to set up a stage screen and a phone remote

Updated 2026-11-20

A stage screen shows the preacher and the singers what is on the projector, what comes next, the clock and a countdown. The congregation never sees it. A phone remote lets someone run the order of service from anywhere in the hall.

The stage screen

Open Tools → Stage. The stage screen shows:

  • Now: the slide on the projector, exactly as the hall sees it, with speaker notes from PowerPoint or PDF slides.
  • Next: the next slide, or the next item in the plan.
  • Clock and timers: the time of day and a speaker countdown that turns green, amber and then red, and counts the overtime.
  • Messages from the desk, such as "5 minutes left", with an optional chime.
  • The order of service, with who leads each part.

Layouts: Full, Words (very large type for singers and the preacher), Slides and Timer. There is a mirror option for teleprompter glass.

Connect a stage screen

Another monitor on the PCTools → Stage → Open stage screen
A TV with Chromecast or Google TVCast stage screen
A phone, tablet or laptopTurn on Phones & laptops, then scan the QR code, or open ssdesk.online/stage and type the room code. No app and no account.
Church Wi-Fi with no internetRun the free companion on the church PC; screens open the address it shows.

Slides go straight from the media desk to the stage screen, encrypted. Someone who only has the room code cannot show fake slides.

Phone remote for the order of service

  1. On the PC, open the projector once (press Present).
  2. Open Tools → Stage → Phone remote and scan the QR code with the phone.
  3. Enter the control PIN shown on the PC.
  4. The phone shows the plan with a Show button per part, big Back and Next part buttons, the slide arrows, and Black, Logo and Clear.

The phone and the PC connect directly on the church Wi-Fi; the internet is only used to find each other. Wrong PINs are limited, so a guest cannot take over the screen.
