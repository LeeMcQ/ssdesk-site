How to run a church service with no internet

Updated 2026-11-20

Church buildings often have weak or no internet, and data costs money. Sabbath Service Desk is built so that a whole service runs with no internet at all. Here is how to set it up once, and how to check it every week.

Once: store everything on the church PC

  1. Open ssdesk.online in Chrome or Edge on the church PC, with internet.
  2. Keep it open for a minute. A card at the bottom shows the progress and then Saved on this device. The Bibles, hymns, hymn music, fonts and slide tools are now on the PC.
  3. Press Install. The app gets its own icon and window, and opens with no internet.
  4. Press Protect data when it is offered, so the browser never clears the app to free disk space.
  5. In the setup's last screen, Ready for Sabbath, press Download all for the Bibles and hymnbooks of your church's languages.
  6. Import your slides and videos before the Sabbath. They are kept on the device.

Every Friday evening: the two-minute check

  1. Open Sabbath Service Desk with the internet on and wait for ✔ Ready offline at the top.
  2. Tap the badge: every line should have a tick. Apply any waiting update now, not tomorrow.
  3. Switch the internet off. Close Sabbath Service Desk completely and open it again.
  4. Show a Bible verse, a hymn with music and the first slide of the plan on the projector.
  5. Switch the internet back on, or leave it off for the service.

What keeps the service safe

  • No updates during the Sabbath. From Friday midday to Saturday night, and while something is on screen, an update never takes over by itself.
  • Updates cannot half-happen. A new version downloads completely first; if it is interrupted, the working version stays.
  • It repairs itself. Every half hour with internet, missing files are fetched again.
  • One problem does not stop the rest. If one tab fails, it offers Try again, and every other tab keeps working.
  • Online buttons hide when there is no internet, so a volunteer never presses something that cannot work.

Phones, stage screens and no internet at all

A phone remote or a stage screen on a phone connects over the church Wi-Fi. To do this with no internet at all, run the free companion program on the church PC. It also makes an emergency USB copy: the whole app in one file that starts on any Windows or Linux PC, together with a backup of your plans and slides.

Signs that something is wrong: the badge shows ⚠ files missing (press it and choose Check & repair, with internet), or the projector picture disappears when you point at the Windows taskbar (turn off Windows Peek: Win+R, sysdm.cpl → Advanced → Performance Settings → untick Enable Peek).
