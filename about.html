Free church presentation software for Adventist media teams

Updated 2026-11-20

Sabbath Service Desk is free church presentation software for Seventh-day Adventist media teams. It puts the Bible, hymns with music, slides, video and announcements on the projector or TV, and gives the platform a stage screen. It installs from your web browser, works with no internet once installed, and runs on an old church PC or a phone.

What it puts on the screen

  • The Bible. Type John 3:16 or Ps 23 and it is ready. Two translations side by side, text that fills the screen, long passages split into readable slides.
  • Hymns with music. Find a hymn by number or words. One stanza per slide, the chorus repeated for you, and the hymn music played from the device with no internet. The words change just before the music reaches them, so people can read ahead.
  • Slides. PowerPoint, PDF and pictures, kept on the device. A WhatsApp poster can be pasted straight in.
  • Video and music. A player with playlists, fades and volume boost, and background music that keeps playing under the words.
  • Banners and announcements. Welcome, tithe and offering, countdown, Sabbath sunset and more, filled in with your church name, the date and the sunset time. A pre-service loop with a "Service starts in" clock.
  • Web pages and YouTube, when the church has internet.

Made for the Adventist service

  • Ready-made service plans: Divine Service, Sabbath School, Vespers / AY, Communion, Prayer Meeting, Thirteenth Sabbath, Baptism, Child Dedication, Pathfinder and Adventurer Investiture, and Week of Prayer.
  • Sabbath times worked out with no internet from the church's location, and a "Happy Sabbath" greeting from Friday sunset.
  • Key verses for the 28 Fundamental Beliefs and about 40 sermon topics, in English or Afrikaans.
  • Sabbath School lesson banners, when switched on in Settings.
  • No updates during the Sabbath: from Friday midday to Saturday night, and while something is on screen, an update never takes over by itself.

Easy for the volunteer at the desk

  • Next and Live. Prepare on your own screen; nothing reaches the congregation until you press Present.
  • Basic mode shows the order of service with a Show button per part and big Back and Next buttons.
  • Black, Logo and Clear are always one key away. A presentation clicker works without set-up.
  • Phone remote. Run the order of service from a phone on the church Wi-Fi, protected by a PIN.
  • Stage screen. The preacher sees the clock, a countdown, what comes next, speaker notes and messages from the desk. The congregation never sees it.
  • Low-power mode for old PCs and 1–2 GB phones switches on by itself.

Start in five steps

  1. On the church PC, open ssdesk.online in Chrome or Edge, with internet.
  2. Press Install when the card at the bottom offers it, and wait for Saved on this device.
  3. Follow the short setup: your church name, town and service languages, your screens and hymn music.
  4. Connect the projector as an extended screen (Windows: Win+P → Extend), then press Present.
  5. On Friday evening do the two-minute check in Run a church service with no internet.

If you are choosing a church presenter

Well-known church presenters include EasyWorship and ProPresenter, which are paid subscriptions, and OpenLP and FreeShow, which are free. They are general tools for any church, and many churches use them well. Sabbath Service Desk is narrower: it is free, it runs in the browser, and it is built around the Adventist service, with Adventist service plans, Sabbath times, hymns with music and a quiet Sabbath with no updates.

What it does not do: it is not a desktop program you download (the free optional companion adds a program for the church PC), and putting words on a projector works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Questions and answers

What is Sabbath Service Desk?

A free church presenter made for Seventh-day Adventist media teams. It shows the Bible, hymns with music, slides, video and announcements on a projector or TV, and gives the platform a stage screen with a clock and timers. It runs in a web browser at ssdesk.online and installs from there.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There are no adverts and no payment. A few optional online extras, such as AI pictures from a provider you choose, are run by other companies and may have their own charges.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once it is installed. The first visit needs the internet to store the app on the device. After that a whole service runs with no signal. Extra Bibles and hymnbooks, web pages and updates need the internet when you add them.

What do I need to run it?

A computer with Chrome or Edge is best for a projector or TV on an extended screen. An old church PC is fine: a low-power mode switches on by itself on weak devices. Phones and tablets can run it too, or act as a remote or a stage screen.

Which Bibles and hymnbooks does it have?

Built in: the King James Version, the World English Bible and the Afrikaans 1933/53 Bible, with English and Afrikaans hymnbooks that play hymn music. Hymnbooks in more languages, such as isiXhosa, isiZulu, Sesotho, Setswana, Shona, Swahili, French, Portuguese and Spanish, can be added once and then work offline. Licensed Bibles such as the NIV or ESV are added by each church from its own file.

Can I show PowerPoint slides?

Yes. PowerPoint, PDF and pictures are turned into slides once and kept on the device. Click animations in PowerPoint are played one step per click. Speaker notes show on the stage screen.

Does it work with a presentation clicker?

Yes. Common clickers (Logitech, Targus, Norwii and others) work without set-up, and a clicker test shows what each button sends.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in is optional. The app has no adverts and no tracking cookies; see the privacy notice.

Is it an official Seventh-day Adventist Church product?

No. It is an independent, free tool made by Lee McQuire of WiFi vs The Word Ministries in Malmesbury, South Africa, for church media teams. It is not made or endorsed by the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
