Pictures for sharing Sabbath Service Desk (ssdesk.online). All are made by tools/media/make_media.py from screenshots of the real app (dark theme, King James Bible text only, no hymn words, no Adventist Church logos: only the app's own icon and the Advent Sans font). og-1200x630.png is the link preview (Open Graph / Twitter card) that WhatsApp, Facebook and others show when the address is pasted, and it is kept under 300 KB on purpose; og-square-1200.png is the same message as a square. screenshot-wide.png (1280x720, the Bible tab with John 3:16 live) and screenshot-narrow.png (750x1334, the phone layout) are plain screenshots for the website, app listings and the press kit. facebook-cover-820x312.png, linkedin-1200x627.png, youtube-banner-2560x1440.png, instagram-square-1080.png, instagram-portrait-1080x1350.png and whatsapp-status-1080x1920.png are the sizes those places ask for (the Facebook and YouTube text sits in the middle so phones do not crop it, and the WhatsApp text keeps clear of the top and bottom bars). a4-poster.pdf is a one-page poster for a church noticeboard, and promo-15s.mp4 is a silent 17-second square video. To make them again after the app changes, run "python tools/media/make_media.py" from the repo root (needs playwright, Pillow, Chrome and, for the video, ffmpeg; no internet). The files qr-ssdesk.* and share.js in this folder belong to the marketing work, not to this script.
